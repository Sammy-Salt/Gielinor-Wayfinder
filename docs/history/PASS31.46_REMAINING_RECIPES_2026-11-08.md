## Decisions and limits
- Tiaras and staves exist only for the 12 altars that map a Talisman, Tiara and Staff by name. OMNI and ASTRAL cannot be made by the enchanting listener, so they are not recipes.
- Wine records the bad-wine chance only in the method text (the pulse picks bad wine on 1 of 3); xp is granted on success.
- Brewables are modelled by the main ingredient only (hops/ingredient ×4, ×1 for Axeman's Folly and Kelda). Water, ale yeast, vat and barrel stages are not separate rows.
- Not extracted because they are not skill recipes or have no catalog items: coconut cracking, tea and teapot handling, nettle tea, Fremennik/Kelda decanting.
